# Read, think, connect, retain

## Guided loop

Select the current question, relevant notes and original passages. Explain the
argument, comparable evidence, tensions and gaps. Reuse before proposing another
explanation. Retention follows [review](review-contract.md); originals stay original.

## Citations and lean metadata

For new scientific derived notes, use numbered Vancouver citations such as [1] in order of first appearance, unless the owner or library requires another style. Reuse a source's number within the document; citation numbers are independent of MOC or synthesis addresses.

Keep complete Vancouver references and verified, clickable DOI links when available in the document's References section, not a second formatted bibliography in frontmatter. Preserve original titles and identifiers. Use only verified bibliographic details; do not invent a DOI, journal abbreviation, volume or pages, and do not treat missing DOI as poor evidence. Identify what was actually consulted (abstract, section or full text). A citation of an article does not imply full-text review. Original captures remain unchanged.

Keep only metadata required by the permitted writer, numbering profile, local views and useful provenance. Preserve stable identity, recognized maturity, primary association, reservations and existing evidence identifiers/version/locators in their supported structures. Do not invent a new citation registry or flatten a schema ad hoc. Omit unknown-author placeholders and generator-model fields by default in new notes; preserve real authorship and never remove protected legacy fields through a bypass. Do not generate a manually maintained updated field unless an explicit editorial-date purpose requires it; retain dates consumed by local views. Avoid a second fixed declaration of mutable address/primary association in prose; use ordinary navigation links instead. Existing notes change only through their library's exact-review/save boundary, never bulk conversion.

## Idea emergence

Raw originals remain unchanged. Only authored notes develop:

- **Semilla** (`seed`): bounded question, observation or hypothesis with uncertainty.
- **Síntesis** (`synthesis`): explicit organizing argument connecting ideas/evidence,
  preserving tensions and unresolved gaps.
- **Evergreen** (`evergreen`): reusable explanation with checked central claims,
  counterevidence and scope; still correctable.

This is not a source-count or link-count threshold. Trace claims to originals;
state what a proposed connection adds. Explain current/proposed maturity; new
evidence may justify reopening or correction through review. Reuse the same note
for continuous ideas. Missing maturity stays unknown. A retained seed need not
be Evergreen; publication permission is separate.

## Optional living-synthesis profile

Opt into this profile for a bounded collection under its local rules; it does not
change Core's [source/note/map model](ontology.md) or require maps for all libraries.
A living synthesis is one integrated, revisable explanation, not a catalog of
seeds or a collection of summaries. Reuse a useful explanation before adding one.

Seeds may be fragments inside a draft or synthesis: no compulsory atomic file for
each observation. Give the synthesis an organizing argument, meaningful sections,
exact citations to original titles/passages and explicit connections beside the
claims they support or challenge. Cite a heading/section when that is the useful
reading unit; preserve referenced headings or reconcile their incoming links when
revising. Keep quotations, paraphrases and inference distinguishable. Record
intellectual lineage, tensions and open questions in the argument itself, not a
separate trace registry. Originals and their evidence versions remain unchanged.

For example, a fictional synthesis about shade and surface temperature could
integrate observations under “Comparison conditions” and “Unresolved differences”.
Its argument would explain why the conditions matter, cite the exact original
sections and retain contrary observations; listing those observations alone would
not constitute a synthesis. This illustrates structure, not scientific validation.

### Grow thematic routes from useful explanations

Every retained synthesis in this profile associates with one **thematic primary
MOC** (map of content): reuse an existing map or co-propose the synthesis and its
map bottom-up. The map provides a reading route with a meaningful theme and links;
it is not an agent, type catalog, folder requirement or maturity level. Generated
inventories are catalogs, not thematic MOCs; technical role `map` alone never
makes an object eligible for this numbering. Do not
prebuild a taxonomy before there is useful content to organize. Seeds need no
address or compulsory MOC; classification by map/tag remains optional outside
this scoped profile. Only notes have maturity, independent of review.

Other maps may reference the same synthesis without copying or renumbering it.
Use the library's existing permitted representation for the primary association.
The [optional helper](first-cut-tools.md#optional-living-synthesis-numbering) has an
explicit opt-in field representation; it does not impose that template globally. Optional
[MOC-scoped addresses](../examples/numbered-notes/README.md#moc-scoped-synthesis-addresses-optional)
are human orientation, not technical identity. Maturity progression alone does not
change that association or address.

Retain changes through the existing [review contract](review-contract.md), not by
chat ingestion or automatic promotion. Adapt to existing folders, schemas and
hosts; Obsidian, K-Plex and Dataview are optional surfaces, not prerequisites.
The [phased adoption plan](implementation.md#prospective-living-synthesis-adoption)
distinguishes implemented bounded mechanics from a future owner-authorized pilot.
