# Review and save

## When to Use

Retain a useful correction/proposal or recover an authorized edit.

## Procedure

1. Prepare complete content/evidence, reusing existing explanations.
2. For [idea emergence](../../docs/knowledge-workflow.md#idea-emergence), explain current
   and proposed maturity; when new evidence undermines an argument, propose correction.
3. Perform the [review contract](../../docs/review-contract.md): actual decision,
   current inputs, permitted writer, recovery and exact readback.

## Pitfalls

[Plain mechanics](../../docs/plain-saving.md) authenticate no consent; missing writer
means proposal-only. Follow [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md).

## Verification

Distinguish proposed, confirmed, saved and managed-approved. Verify the exact version;
fixture writes are not human decisions.
