## Failures and limits

- Copying documentation into the vault created unwanted inferred parents; copy
  Raw/Wiki only.
- Related combined with Contrasts moved the challenge into the friend region.
- After combined note/settings edits a transient view showed body links as
  children until the index was rebuilt; incremental convergence there is not
  established. A probe run before workspace restoration failed.
- The `sources` field mapping has structural coverage but its native field was
  not exercised.
- Not run: automatic selected-node delivery, keyboard-highlighted selection,
  other plugin versions, mobile, themes, accessibility, human review/save or
  real source capture.
